The v8 demonstration tongue weights originate from the upstream
n0tmast3r/Qpro-Enhanced-FT release v0.1.10, under its MIT license. They contain
learned weights, not camera recordings. Personal calibration stays local.
models/ava-mouth-encoder.pt is a mouth-camera encoder QFT+ trained on the Ava-256
dataset by Meta Platforms (https://github.com/facebookresearch/ava-256). It is
licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/),
not under LICENSE: non-commercial use only. Changes: new weights learned from
the dataset; no Ava-256 images are included. Cheek, lip, nose and jaw
calibrations trained from it inherit the same terms.
No stock Meta model or firmware is included. The eye patcher uses a compatible
model from the user's own headset; generated models/eye files are private.
